## Research Field 
### Over-the-Air Computation

**Over-the-Air Computation (AirComp)** is an emerging paradigm that seamlessly integrates communication and computation by harnessing the superposition property of wireless multi-access channels to compute target functions directly over the air. By simultaneously aggregating distributed signals, AirComp drastically reduces latency and bandwidth consumption, making it a key enabler for massive data aggregation in next-generation networks. Our laboratory focuses on advancing AirComp across three key frontiers: (1) Performance Optimization, including joint transceiver beamforming, channel synchronization, and integration with reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RIS); (2) Application
Exploration, with an emphasis on federated edge learning, distributed consensus, and massive IoT sensing; and (3) Secure and Private Transmission, developing physical-layer security techniques and privacy-preserving mechanisms against eavesdropping, jamming, and inference attacks.
